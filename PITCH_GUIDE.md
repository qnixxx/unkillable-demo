# Unkillable investor demo — 5 minute walkthrough

## 1. Frame the problem — 30 seconds

**“Most habit products are optimized for good weeks. Unkillable is designed for the week that goes wrong.”**

Show the homepage and the line:

> BECOME HARDER TO BREAK.

Explain the product thesis: resilience is not never failing; it is staying consistent, absorbing disruption, adapting, and returning quickly.

## 2. Show the system — 45 seconds

Open the product dashboard.

Point out:

- Unkillable Score: **82**
- Current streak: **37 days**
- 30-day consistency: **91%**
- Six-pillar balance

The key message: this is not just a list of habits. It is a resilience operating system with a measurable daily loop.

## 3. Demonstrate the daily behavior loop — 60 seconds

Open **Today**.

Complete and undo one commitment. Show that the score/progress responds to actual app state and persists in the browser.

Say:

**“The interface is deliberately calm. The product is trying to make follow-through normal, not turn self-improvement into a game.”**

## 4. Show the differentiator — 60 seconds

Activate **Minimum Day**.

Explain:

**“When the user is overloaded, the product reduces the dose instead of allowing the entire system to collapse.”**

This is the central retention/product philosophy: preserve identity and momentum during disruption.

Then reference **Never Miss Twice**: one missed day is treated as a recovery event, not a broken identity.

## 5. Show compounding value — 60 seconds

Open **Analytics** and move between 7 / 30 / 90 / 365 days.

Then open **Review**.

Explain that longer product usage creates more valuable personal evidence:

- pillar trends;
- recovery patterns;
- consistency history;
- weekly focus;
- proof that the user can depend on themselves.

This gives the product a reason to become more useful over time rather than being abandoned after initial motivation fades.

## 6. Close — 45 seconds

Return to the landing page/pricing.

Suggested close:

**“The wedge is daily resilience and discipline. The broader opportunity is becoming the personal operating system people use to remain dependable across health, work, recovery, and relationships.”**

The current link is intentionally front-end only. The next production layer is authenticated cloud data, subscription infrastructure, and real-user behavioral learning.
